Tilesets load from a URL, a local folder picker, or a persistent File System Access handle. Revit-linked models are auto-detected and split into per-link buildings with feature-level filters, and each building gets its own floor list — clipping-plane cutaways expose the interior of a single level, with a cross-building floor selector to move through the station.

PLATEAU context comes from a catalog picker with a Leaflet map preview, with LOD filtering to suppress duplicate buildings across CityGML levels of detail and per-feature ghost/hide overrides with click-through picking. Shapefile overlays render as flat-height polygons, and File Geodatabase or GeoPackage layers import through GDAL in a web worker, with heuristic matching to buildings and floors.

Indoor networks are authored directly on the model: stairs, escalators, elevators, slopes, and moving walkways connect across floors through a floor-to-floor connect mode, with waypoint placement along stair geometry and GeoJSON export of the authored connectors.

Sessions back up and restore automatically with snapshots and a visual diff between states, and publishing bundles the tilesets and session metadata into a package the server mirrors and shares by link.
